**F1 — high — Closed m52 trails release exclusion while their temporal transition entries can still be emitted.**

Primary location: `source-b1cfc8a/Source/AnomalyCapture/Private/AnomalyCaptureSubsystem.cpp:6314`. The corresponding admission decision is `source-b1cfc8a/Source/AnomalyInjector/Public/AnomalyExclusion.h:121`.

Evidence: `QueryExclusionTrail` scans only `Async->Trails` and `Async->CarriedTrails` (ACS:6314, ACS:6315). A closed trail returns `Closed`, overriding even a nonempty game-thread Restoring set; `Closed` does not exclude (EXCL:79, EXCL:90, EXCL:121). However, closing the render-truth window is distinct from exhausting the label-sync off window. The default temporal off window is eight captured frames (LABEL:14, LABEL:235). The trail can close after its confirmation run and fence while that off window remains (WINDOW:380).

After detach, `AddDetachedTransitionCandidates` still reads the transition track and carried tail fires (ACS:7389, ACS:7395). `ResolveDetachedTransitionCandidates` emits an m52 transition entry while `bOff` remains true (ACS:7433, ACS:7438). Tail tracks can also be carried and adopted independently of an open trail (ACS:7256, ACS:7299). Thus a closed or tail-only carried m52 event can coexist with a newly admitted, on-screen, FireWindow-labelled m53 event. For example, closure shortly after the last m52 member does not consume the remaining eight-frame off window; an m53 fire in that remainder creates the combination.

This is counted as an overlap by the DC's own implementation: ACS:6358 includes transition-only m52 entries, ACS:6367 includes detached `TransitionFires`, and ACS:6374 increments `texcorrupt_m52_overlap_frames` when m53 is labelled. The m52 transition is legitimate label-sync output; erasing it would damage the label product. The defect is the mismatch between the exclusion's release condition and EXCL-DATA's broader prohibition of any m52 entry beside a labelled m53 entry. Plan section 3.1's predicate alone does not establish section 3.7's EXCL-DATA guarantee.

Proposed fix: make opposite-family admission account for an outstanding m52 temporal off tail, including a carried tail with no attached trail, if the no-coentry guarantee is retained. Keep m52 label membership unchanged. Handle the explicitly accepted `restore_unresolved` exception separately; do not silently turn that ruling into an indefinite wait. Add a state-sequence check that closes/detaches m52 inside its off window and then attempts m53, including the tail-only next-run case.

Next-window exposure: **on the EXCL-A adopted-trail/release path with temporal AA on**, and consequently EXCL-DATA. Manifestation depends on the first successful m53 frame landing before the off window expires; a routine leg with a long gap can miss it. The tail-only run-boundary case is not explicitly forced by the current recipe. With the normal AA-off setting, the effective temporal off window is zero and this particular cause disappears. Isolated fixture/MainWorld m53 legs, EXCL-B without prior m52 history, G-SEED without m52 history, and census do not by themselves produce this state.

**F2 — high — A late m52 reopen can occur after m53 admission; the reopen handler neither prevents the overlap nor consistently preserves the old m52 event.**

Primary location: `source-b1cfc8a/Source/AnomalyCapture/Private/AnomalyCaptureSubsystem.cpp:6504`. Related consequences are at `source-b1cfc8a/Source/AnomalyCapture/Private/AnomalyCaptureSubsystem.cpp:6660` and `:6744`.

Evidence: the new exclusion treats `bClosed` as releasable even though closed is not terminal in the inherited m52 lifecycle. `BeginFire` calls `DetachClosedTrailsForNextFire` before attempting the new fire (ACS:5088, ACS:5099). That uses `TryDetach(..., true)` (ACS:6504), which explicitly permits detach with previously armed receipts still unprocessed (WINDOW:331, WINDOW:337). A later HELD or UNKNOWN receipt reopens the trail, including after detach (WINDOW:304, WINDOW:346).

This is an existing, concrete window-model sequence, not a hypothetical unordered read: `tools/m52_window_selftest.cpp:410` arms 500–503, establishes a closure fence at 503, arms 504, closes on receipt 503 with 504 outstanding, and then reopens on HELD receipt 504. The test source establishes the allowed transition; its C++ executable was not rerun in this review. An opposite-family fire admitted between closure and that late receipt is not retroactively protected by checking the provider on the next admission.

On a different target, `HandleTrailReopens` adds the old m52 fire to later pending snapshots and marks their missing products unrecoverable (ACS:6717, ACS:6731). Its own diagnostic says these frames become UNKNOWN and labelled; already-written frames cannot be repaired (ACS:6753). It reserves the old actor and refuses m52 (ACS:6746), but does not resolve an already-active m53 fire. Pending m53 snapshots can therefore acquire an m52 label as well, or have irrecoverable missing m52 products.

On the same target, the alternate branch tests for **any** new fire rather than a new m52 hold (ACS:6660). It marks the old trail closed/detached again and suppresses its reopen (ACS:6668). An m53 material corruption is not evidence that a held-mip receipt belongs to a new m52 hold. This branch can hide the reopened m52 event instead of merely reporting an overlap.

Proposed fix: introduce an admission barrier for the opposite family that finalizes all already-armed m52 receipts and the relevant label tail before releasing it. The existing next-fire force-detach shortcut is insufficient for that guarantee. Preserve the m52 products through the barrier. If reopen after admission remains possible, define explicit handling/invalidation of affected frames and the already-active m53 event; blocking only future fires cannot repair already-captured frames. Restrict any same-target hold-crosstalk suppression to evidence that actually supports that explanation. Add an integration sequence combining the existing late-receipt window case with m53 admission, on both same and different targets.

Next-window exposure: **on EXCL-A's async adopted-trail release path, with AA on or off**. The stated targets are different, so its different-target branch is directly relevant. The recipe does not deliberately force a late HELD/UNKNOWN receipt after release; passing the ordinary leg would not cover this race. The same-target variant is outside that recipe. Pure m53 captures, EXCL-B's ordinary forward check, G-SEED without an m52 trail, and census do not exercise the producer. The inherited m52 logic is not a merge-resolution regression; the DC exclusion assumes more finality from it than it provides.

**F3 — high — An invalid raw re-Apply can remove the active record without ending the active effect, defeating the ApplyAnomaly exclusion backstop.**

Proposed fix: preserve or reconcile the existing record on refusal when the anomaly remains active, and make the exclusion predicate robust to a lost record. Avoid curing stale records by treating a missing record as proof that an active effect cannot exist. Proposed fix: give terminal capture cleanup an explicit operation to cancel pending commits and complete pending restores for the world before final accounting and return. Keep the delay at an ordinary in-run BeginRevert so the intended can-fail arm still works. Add a stop/cap-before-deadline followed immediately by a new-run check.
