## Chat's ruling: cut the checks down to what the product needs
Most of the ~33 checks were written to make the home bench's proofs airtight (identity pixel-exact, full residency, exact mip-chain shape, all-or-nothing footprints), with "when in doubt, refuse" as the default. 1. **the label could disagree with the picture:** the effect is invisible or partial while labelled, or visible while unlabelled;
2. **the effect could land on the wrong object,** or visibly change something outside the target;
3. **the game could crash, leak or exceed a hard memory cap;**
4. **revert could leave residue.**

**Every other check becomes non-blocking:** logged and counted as a note, and it no longer refuses the object. Classify all ~33 in a table: **keep (which of 1–4) / non-blocking / removed**, with a one-line reason each.
