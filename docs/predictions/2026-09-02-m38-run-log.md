## 0. THE TWO POLICY DECLARATIONS THE GATES DEPEND ON

### 0.1 FLUSH POLICY — **PER-LINE WRITE-THROUGH**

Every accepted line is formatted, written, and **`Flush()`ed to the file handle immediately**. There
is no user-space buffering held across lines.

**Why it is declared here rather than discovered:** gate (iii) leg 2 hard-kills the process, and what
survives is a *direct consequence of this choice*. Under per-line write-through the bytes have
already reached the OS file cache, which outlives the process, so **a hard kill loses nothing that
was logged before it** — at most a torn final line. Under buffering the answer would be *"everything
since the last 4 KB boundary is gone"*, which is unpredictable and would make gate (iii) leg 2
ungradeable.

**Cost, stated rather than hidden:** one `Flush()` per accepted line. On a no-flags leg that is a few
dozen lines per run; with census+mask ON the bench emits roughly ten lines per captured frame, so
order 10³ flushes per run. It is a diagnostic file, and gate (iv) is a *content* comparison, not a
timing one.

### 0.2 CLOSE ORDERING — and therefore the `FINISHED`-line prediction

`EndRunLog()` is **idempotent** and is called from **four** sites:

| # | site | file:line (pre-`m38`) | why there |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `FinishRun`, cancelled branch, **immediately before** `DeleteDirectory` | `AnomalyCaptureSubsystem.cpp:3038` | a held handle blocks the delete |
| 2 | `FinishRun`, **very last statement** | after `:3157` | so the whole of `FinishRun` is inside the file |
| 3 | `Deinitialize`, after `StopRun()` | `:488` | world teardown |
| 4 | `~FAnomalyRunLog` | — | defensive only; must never be the one that does the work |

🎯 **PREDICTION P-FIN — `=== Capture run FINISHED` LANDS *INSIDE* `anomaly_log.txt`.** It is logged
at `:3030`, site 2 closes at the end of `FinishRun`, and `:3030` precedes that. **So do** the mask
summary block (`:3057+`), the pacing line (`:2705`), the tickpin restore (`:3138`) and the
auto-resume (`:3153`). ⛔ **Recorded as an OBSERVATION, not graded** — chat ruled it an observation
because it follows from the ordering rather than testing anything.

📌 **Corollary, also predicted:** on a **cancelled-before-focus** run the file is closed at site 1 and
is then **deleted with `RunDir`** — correct behaviour (no session, no artifacts), and not one of the
gates.

### GATE (v) — the verbosity knob, both directions, **in one leg**

**Leg:** normal config, no flags, `IAI.Capture.RunLogVerbose 1`, targeted `blinking`.

**PREDICTED, part 1 — the raise works and the toggle lines are captured.**
`anomaly_log.txt` contains `blinking toggle ->` lines, **exactly 3 per blinking event**, in the order
**`HIDDEN · VISIBLE · HIDDEN`**, each event's three followed by
`IAI.Revert 'blinking' -> reverted.` **Exact counts for this config**: `2 4 8 4 0`, 90-frame cap
⇒ lead-in `0-3` and event windows `4-11`, `16-23`, `28-35`, `40-47`, `52-59`, `64-71`, `76-83`,
`88-89`. The eighth burst is truncated by the frame cap, so **7 complete events × 3 = 21 toggle
lines**, plus **0–2 further toggle lines** from the truncated eighth burst and the drain tail
(⚠ its exact count depends on where the cap lands and is **NOT** pre-declared — it is read, not
graded). ⛔ **The graded part is the per-event triple and its order on the COMPLETE events.**
🚨 **A mismatch here is a FINDING, reported raw, NOT fixed** — it is Task 1's checkable expectation
meeting a measurement for the first time.

**PREDICTED, part 2 — the restore works, proven BOTH WAYS inside the same leg.** Method, declared
here so it cannot be chosen after the fact: a **paired probe with a unique token**.

- Immediately **after** the raise, one `UE_LOG(LogAnomaly, Verbose, …)` emits
  `RUNLOG-VERBOSE-PROBE raised=1`.
- At `EndRunLog`, the prior verbosity is **restored first**, and then the identical
  `UE_LOG(LogAnomaly, Verbose, …)` emits `RUNLOG-VERBOSE-PROBE raised=0`.
- Both are emitted while the device is still registered.

⇒ **PREDICTED: `anomaly_log.txt` contains `RUNLOG-VERBOSE-PROBE raised=1` EXACTLY ONCE and
`RUNLOG-VERBOSE-PROBE raised=0` ZERO TIMES.** The first proves the raise reached the log; the second
proves the restore did. 🚨 **This is `G96` inside one leg: a guard that only ever passes is not a
guard, and a "restore" that is never shown to suppress anything is not a restore.**
**Plus the two echoes**, both at `Log` verbosity so they are unconditional: a `VERBOSITY RAISED` line
naming the saved value, and a `VERBOSITY RESTORED` line naming the value put back.

**Also predicted:** a leg **without** the knob contains **zero** `blinking toggle ->` lines — the
already-measured baseline (this box's packaged log, session 068 §1.3.2).
