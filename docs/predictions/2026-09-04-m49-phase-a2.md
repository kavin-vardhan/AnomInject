### §1.1 Two deliberate deviations from journal 071 §8.1, declared before the legs

- **The two can-fail levers live in `AnomalyCaptureSubsystem.cpp`, not `AnomalyInjectorSubsystem.cpp`.**
  Both are keyed on **`session_index`**, and that clock is capture-side. A lever keyed on it inside
  the injector would have to invent a second clock, and two clocks that must agree is the shape
  journal 068 §1.4.5 already made this project pay for.
- **The picker exclusion is applied in `ClassifyRenderableVisibleLive`, NOT in
  `IsRenderableComponent`.** Putting it in `IsRenderableComponent` — the `G33` chokepoint the
  foliage exclusion uses — would make the census stop COUNTING translucent components as renderable
  at all, so `TranslucentOnly == Renderable` could never hold and
  `census_excluded_translucent` would read a permanent 0. **That is a silent regression of an
  existing diagnostic**, and `G96` says a counter that cannot fire is blindness. The classifier is
  the shared entry point both live picker paths already use, so the `IAI.DumpVisible` set-identity
  property is preserved.

### §1.2 The one argument that differs between the two consumers, and why

`IsTranslucentOnlyComponent` takes `bAllowCustomDepthOptIn`. **The census passes its own
`bIncludeTranslucentCustomDepthWriters` (default `false`); the picker passes `true`.** The
PREDICATE is shared; the ARGUMENT differs on purpose, because the two are asking different
questions: the census asks "is this in my measurable population under my configured policy", the
picker asks "can the target mask measure this at all", and a translucent material that opts into
writing custom depth **can** be measured. ⛔ This is stated rather than left to be discovered from a
diff.

### §3.5 Leg B — does `MPP_Outline` draw on OUR tags?

**Design (the only one that isolates tagging), pre-declared:**
- Host: Lyra, census ON, **`IAI.Bench.CensusMaskDump N`**.
- Pairs are **consecutive CAPTURED frames** `(A, B)` with **identical `view.origin` AND
  `view.rot`** and **`anomaly_present == false` on BOTH** — so no anomaly appearance change exists
  anywhere in the pair, which is exactly the confound §5.2 of journal 073 could not remove.
- **Treatment pair:** some actor X has a dumped silhouette on one side and is not census-tagged on
  the other. **Control pair:** a pose-matched consecutive negative pair with no census tag change.
- **≥5 treatment pairs and ≥5 control pairs**, or the reading is reported as **NO POWER** and no
  verdict is offered.
- Regions from X's dumped silhouette: **INTERIOR** (silhouette minus a 2 px erosion),
  **RING** (dilation by r minus silhouette, at r = 2 / 6 / 12 / 20 px), **AMBIENT** (rest of frame).
- The instrument is proven against a known answer BEFORE its verdict is read (a `missing_texture`
  onset must read INTERIOR ≫ 0 — journal 073 §5.2's control, which caught PIL's
  `multiply = a*b/255` annihilating the diff).

**The three verdicts, and the reading each requires:**

| verdict | required reading |
|---|---|
| **DRAWS** | RING at r=2 is ≥ 3× the CONTROL pairs' RING at r=2, and **decays to within the control band by r ≈ 12 px** — a thin outline is thin, so widening the ring must dilute it |
| **DOES NOT DRAW** | RING ≈ AMBIENT ≈ the control band at every r, with the control band quoted as the noise floor |
| **UNDECIDABLE** | anything else — in particular a ring that is strong AND does not decay, which is what journal 073 measured and which is consistent with an indirect-light response rather than an outline |

⛔ **`G120`: no mechanism is asserted in any branch.** ⛔ **A2b's refuse-or-warn rule is decided
from this reading; if the verdict is UNDECIDABLE, no rule is written.**
