## AMENDMENT 1 (2026-08-23, BEFORE any G-W run — G-A/G-B untouched)

Caught at FIXTURE-PREP time, before any watcher measurement: the ruled watcher expression
`fps_effective = (N−1)/(t_wall span)` carries the SAME settle-gap disease as the withdrawn plugin
formula. The gaps between bursts are real wall time that is deliberately uncaptured, so the span
under-reads every healthy gapped session: the fixture source (banked `M30_FPS30`, certified
healthy, ratio ≈ 1.000) reads **22.99 measured vs 30 stamped** — a 23 % disagreement that would
fire the loud line and "correct" every healthy client video to play ~30 % slow. The estimator is
therefore **1/median(consecutive `t_wall` delta) over matched rows** — in-burst deltas are the
majority, gap deltas are outliers the median ignores; every other ruled semantic (2 % band, one
behaviour, loud line, vacuity fallbacks) is unchanged. **Flagged to chat as ratify-or-revert; the
deviation is from the ruled EXPRESSION, not the ruled intent.** Robustness limit, named: a config
whose gaps outnumber its in-burst intervals would defeat the median; the shipped default
(`2 4 8 4 0`) is in-burst-majority by a wide margin.

G-W expectations restated under the median estimator:
(a) healthy copy ⇒ median ≈ 1/30 s ⇒ measured ≈ 30 within 2 % ⇒ SILENT, encodes at 30.
(b) t_wall stretched ×2 ⇒ measured ≈ 15.0 vs 30 ⇒ LOUD line, encodes at ≈ 15.0 (within 2 %).
(c)/(d) unchanged (fallback + note).

### AMENDMENT 2 AS SHIPPED — two changes, both flagged ratify-or-revert

**(2a) ESTIMATOR := IN-BURST MEAN.** Keep only the intervals whose GAME-time delta is one tick
(`Δt < 1.5/target_fps`, read from `labels.jsonl`'s own `t`), then `n / Σ Δt_wall` over those.
Gap-immune **by construction from artifact evidence** rather than by a structural guess, and
**spike-SENSITIVE** because it is a mean, not a median — it is the same quantity the plugin's
honest stamp measures, which is what makes it a cross-check rather than a rival estimator.
Measured agreement with the plugin on all four banked sessions: **≤ 0.08 %**. Fallback: rows
without usable `t` ⇒ median rule (AMENDMENT 1), named in the log line.

**(2b) AN HONEST STAMP IS NEVER OVERRIDDEN.** When the session's `run_summary` carries
`game_clock_speed_ratio` (⇒ an m33+ build, whose stamp has been gated end to end), a disagreement
is reported as a **NOTE and the stamp WINS**. Pre-m33 sessions keep the override, which is the
only regime the cross-check was ever built for (a blind stamp). **Rationale, and it is the lesson
of this defect: the cross-check existed because the stamp could be blind; m33 removed the
blindness, so an overriding watcher becomes a second source of truth that can only add error —
and it just did, in the field, on the delivery build.** Two estimator formulas have now been wrong
in three days; a third one arbitrating over a gated instrument is a risk with no remaining upside.
⚠ **This narrows the ruled "one behaviour" to one behaviour PER REGIME, keyed on artifact
evidence (not on config or a flag), and it is a SEMANTIC deviation from the ruling — ratify or
revert.**

📌 **NAMED LIMITATION, recorded not cured:** a session whose in-burst intervals are spiky AND
whose `t` is unusable falls back to the median and stays spike-blind. Also **OPEN AND OWNED BY
CHAT:** if the Concorde in-burst mean disagrees with the plugin's sustained, the excess is
gap-side, not spike-side — i.e. the settle ticks are the slow ones — and *"which number is the
true playback rate"* becomes a semantics question the field data will pose but this amendment does
not answer. (2b) makes that question non-urgent by refusing to act on it unilaterally.
