## 4. Findings and deviations (for chat)
1. **The down-pop is unreachable on these paths.** A re-hold needs coverage ≥ 7 % below 0.85 × the swap size (about
   0.935 × the first-positive size), which a straight retreat from N2 never reaches while the engine is finer than the
   held LOD. C4 shows the up-swap only. Decision for chat/owner: keep the down-pop as built (on by default) or change
   its rule; a geometry that shows it would need a much larger target or a slower switch.
2. **The bench copies can never give an event.** A scale-1 copy of `SM_LodFix_N` switches at 0.05, beyond 17.8 m; its
   7 % size is at about 3 m, ~1,970 frames later at walking speed. Under B2 (look ahead to the later of the two sizes,
   90-frame cap) it is refused `at_finest_lod` on the whole path. Ruling B2 asked that the copies "move onto the path's
   end"; they did (1700, ∓180), and they serve as a refusal reading only. Every distance prediction uses the second
   pair, so **no distance clip is possible before the cook carries the second pair.**
3. **Yield consequence of B2 on real content:** a distance pop needs a mesh whose switch lies near its 7 % size; a mesh
   that switches while small is refused unless the camera closes fast enough. Not a defect; a property of the rule.
4. **Deviation:** distance frames-ahead uses the closing speed in distance (`(D − D × S / Target) / (closing × dt)`),
   not the linear screen-size rate, which under-counted by up to 3× and gave arming windows under one cycle.
5. **Behaviour change (S1):** a hold on a dithered mesh keeps the event open for `lod.TemporalLag` + 0.05 s after the
   swap, so the render probe can see a crossfade.
6. **N2:** the one frame where the engine is past the held LOD is flagged `past_held` (`lod_subvisible`).
7. **"C3 must trigger"** was read as the C3 parameters on the fixed bench geometry; the old copy geometry is refused.
