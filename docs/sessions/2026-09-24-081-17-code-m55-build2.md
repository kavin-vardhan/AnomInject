## 5. The Lyra high-water, read from the census (no mechanism claimed)

The 20 `HIGH-WATER` lines rise 8 → 197 MB across the 16-second capture. The gap between the latest
issued index and the cursor grows 12 → 53, pending colours grow 1 → 21, masks stay at 0–7 (only
labelled frames carry masks), and at 18 of the 20 lines the head is `pending` with **`waits_on`
empty** — every input of the head had arrived and the serial worker had not yet taken it (the other
two lines have the head `in_hand`). Only one
phase is open at the peak (259–265, closing). So this retention is not the closing-phase shape of
Codex's 081-16 counterexample; it is colours accumulating behind a ready head while the backlog grows.
Source fact: `AnomalyChangeStage.cpp` schedules `Work()` and `AnomalyAsyncWriter.cpp` schedules the
PNG encode jobs with the same `Async(EAsyncExecution::ThreadPool, …)`. **Whether the worker waited
behind queued writer jobs is NOT established (G120)** and was not chased. Margin at this peak:
71,443,456 B ≈ 8.6 full 1080p colours. A 1440p run of the same shape would need ≈ 1.78× the colour
bytes, i.e. more than the cap — unexercised, stated, not measured.
