## 2. The fixture fixes — causes, offline proofs, the cook
### 2.1 P-ORC / G-MODE (uv16 15/16, uv64 60/64, normal separation 13.5)
- **Cause, measured on the night's frames:** the engine's filmic tonemapper is not per channel. The missing uv16 cell 13
  (texel 100,220,220) is on screen, unoccluded and flat (range 3/2/2), displays (108,197,196), and the reader's
  per-channel "levels" model predicted (83,199,192): red off by 25 against a tolerance of 24. The same red 100 displays
  as 67, 79, 79 or 108 depending on the other two channels. uv64's missing 44/45/60/61 are the same shape. The interior
  spread of 4 is the default vignette (0.04–0.17 levels/px), the tonemapper's ±1 dither and a bloom halo — not light,
  compression, mips or AA (the tiles are unlit, 8-bit, 1:1, AA off). The normal oracle is lit (a normal- and
  screen-position-dependent term, mechanism not established) and its linear readout lands in the compressed shoulder.
- **Fix (three parts):** (a) legs: `ShowFlag.Tonemapper 0` on P_ORC_UV / P_ORC_N and all six MODE legs, and
  `ShowFlag.Lighting 0` on the normal ones, each read back from the leg's echo or the attempt is INVALID; (b) fixture:
  M_TC_OracleN's readout is `pow(N * 0.5 + 0.5, 2.2)`, so gamma-only display shows the encoded bytes; (c) reader
  (`089-04-oracle.py`): a cell whose colour misses is placed by the fitted geometry (at most a quarter of the cells, only
  when ≥ 75 % were found directly, only if flat and ≥ 32 from every found colour; it never feeds the fit), and
  `judge_session` skips the null-as-modes pass when P-ORC fails (last night's ValueError).
- **Proof offline:** the reader's selftest 39/39 (two identical cells, 20 px apart and 0.5 scale still fail); on the
  night's P_ORC frames uv16 reads 16/16 and uv64 64/64 and **still FAILs on interior**, which only the show flag can fix.
  Parts (a) and (b) cannot be proven offline (a commandlet render is black, G411): the first premise leg decides.
- ⚠ P-ORC's separation floor (32) equals G-MODE's "32 from every other colour" rule: no margin (a threshold, not changed).

### 2.2 G-BIND (INVALID-FIXTURE: the halves could not be located)
- **Cause: the recipe, not the fixture.** S1 put the AA-off arbiter in its BASE for every leg (`082-07-lib.py:458`); the DC
  harness made it a per-leg lever and the four BIND legs lost it (their echoes read TSR 4, Lumen GI 1, Lumen reflections
  1). TSR blends the silhouette edge, lifting the uniform half's range from 8 (S1) to 17 > 16. TC_Layer sits at the same
  pixels in both runs; the DC cells are ≥ 160 px below it.
- **Fix:** the BIND legs carry the arbiter; `bind_gate` reads each BIND leg's echo and returns INVALID-RECIPE for any
  non-zero cvar; every arbiter leg's echo is a validity read-back. No reader change is needed: the S1 bank passes the
  unchanged rows. Offline: the night's four legs read INVALID-RECIPE, the S1 BIND bank reads OK; with the halves located
  (2-px edge excluded) the night's per-frame row passes 57/57 frames (1,104–1,150 of 1,456 px) and the far strip fails on
  the TSR / Lumen halo, which is the recipe cause again.

### 2.3 MIXED — the ruling's premise was not the cause
- The night's decision lines show **slot 1 (M_TC_MixedC, colour-only) already had its colour texture** (`bindings=1`,
  `class=colour`, qualified for uv). The refusal came from **slot 0: `raw=BasicShapeMaterial(len 1)`**, a component
  override left on the spawned actor, so slot 0 resolved to a textureless engine material (`no_textures`). Every DC tile
  carried that override before `set_material` replaced it; TC_Mixed is the one spawned with `override=False`. (The
  engine's basic-shape actor factory sets exactly this override, `ActorFactory.cpp:664`; whether `spawn_actor_from_class`
  takes that path is not established.)
- **Fix:** the new `dcfix` mode empties TC_Mixed's override array; slot 0 resolves to M_TC_MixedN, slot 1 stays the
  colour texture with no normal map (the ruling's intended end state). Adding a base-colour texture to slot 1 alone, as
  the ruling worded it, would not have changed either verdict. **Predictions re-declared before any run:** MIXED_N every
  decision `partial_footprint:1/2:no_normal_map` and 0 APPLIED; MIXED_UV APPLIED, no partial_footprint.
