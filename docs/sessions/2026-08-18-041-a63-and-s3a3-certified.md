## 1. G96, fourth instance — and this one was caught inside an accepted result

**The metric:** `overlap = missing ∩ claimed`. I used `overlap = 0` in journal 039 to conclude *"a
dropped positive frame was never exercised."*

**Why it is blind:** a correctly dropped positive vanishes from the files **and** from the claims, so
the intersection is empty. **Zero overlap is simultaneously the "never tested" reading and the
"tested and handled perfectly" reading.** The metric cannot separate them — in exactly the region it
was introduced to judge.

**The sound discriminator: did the CLAIMED SET SHRINK against the clean run?** Under it, phase 0
genuinely dropped no positive (claims stayed the full canonical 30) and **phase 2 dropped 7**
(claims 23 of 30).

This is the **fourth instance of G96's principle** — an instrument blind in the region it judges —
after fixed-K robust sigma under drift, the ±2 neighbour-window LC on contiguous sets, and
bracket-not-contain floor non-derivability. **The first three were caught before their results were
used. This one was caught inside a result that had already been reported, accepted and committed.**
The escalation matters: a blind metric survives review when its output looks like the answer you
expected.

## 2. A63 — FOCUS-MATCH IS A LEG VALIDITY CONDITION

**Adopted.** Joins the existing A31 validity conditions rather than inventing a mechanism: declared
in advance, crude, loud, leg-invalidating.

> Any leg entering a **cross-binary comparison** must record its focus condition. Two legs are
> **COMPARABLE only if their `start_frame` values match** (equivalently: both acquired focus
> promptly, neither rode the 30 s timeout). If they differ, **the legs are NOT comparable, the
> comparison DOES NOT RUN**, and the leg is **INVALID** and re-run — exactly as an out-of-band ratio
> or a failed luminance floor invalidates a leg today.

**INVALID is not FAILED, and the distinction is what stops this laundering a red:** invalidity is
declared by a **pre-fixed rule**, on a condition **independent of the outcome**, and is decided
**before the comparison is read**. A leg is discarded for *how it ran*, never for *what it showed*.
Re-running an INVALID leg is therefore not selecting a favourable result; re-running a FAILED leg
would be.

**REJECTED: pinning with `IAI.Capture.FocusGate 0` on every leg.** That changes the system under
test — **G93** records focus-gate × fixed-timestep corrupting the camera intermittently, *even at
240 fps with the gate on*. A safety mechanism with a known interaction with the measured quantity is
not disabled to make a comparison convenient.

### 2.2 Correction to journal 039's publish counts

I attributed the ForceMiss legs' large `key_ring_published` (2228 vs 121 on the clean leg) to
*"dropped frames stalling the phase machine."* **Wrong.** The real cause is the focus-gate wait: the
SVE is activated in `StartRun` **before** the focus-gate branch, so it publishes a key for every view
family during the entire 30 s `ArmedPending` wait. Every high-count leg rode the timeout:

```
S3A2_FM4 2098 · S3A3_OFF 2559 · S3A3_P1 2529 · S3A3_P2 2561 · S3A3_P3 2363
```

The counts are an artifact of waiting, not of dropping. **This does not disturb the ForceMiss
verdicts** — those rest on `missed == corrupted`, the file set, and the 1:1 file↔row correspondence,
none of which depend on how many keys were published beforehand.

⚠ **The dropped-positive evidence (`S3A3_P2`) came from a timeout leg.** Its gate criteria are
internal to the single leg — claimed indices with no file behind them, 1:1 correspondence, the
claimed-set-shrink comparison against the clean run's *shape* — so A63 does not apply and the result
stands. Stating it rather than leaving it to be noticed.
