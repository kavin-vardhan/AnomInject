## Part A counting contract, in progress

Family codes: 0 UV, 1 normal, 2 blurry proxy evaluation. Stacks are compiled stack occurrences per component/material slot, each counted once even when it has several layers. VT components and bindings include every compiled virtual binding in the evaluated nonempty opaque mesh footprint. Required counts follow each family's binding requirement. Preallocated stacks are counted even when they have no ordinary UTexture binding; their component format sets remain incomplete.

Format bits: BC5=1, DXT5=2, BC7=4, DXT1=8, BGRA8=16, other=32. A binding/component set is the exact union of all its required VT layer formats. To count complete footprints supported by a proposed set, sum only masks contained in it. Unread data never becomes a supported format. Per-axis address classes: unread=0, wrap=1, clamp=2, mirror=3. Tiles use 0 for unread; borders use -1.

`only_vt`/`only_stack` mean no other observed blocker in the existing all-reasons evaluation. Their `unassessed` subsets explicitly retain its dependency boundary: they are not guaranteed future eligible components. Unnamed required VT bindings are checked independently so a known unnamed sample is never called VT-only merely because the binding evaluator returns at VT.
