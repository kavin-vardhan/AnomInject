### 1.1 What the ordinary path now looks like, so nobody is surprised on Tuesday

- **Frames after a closure carry a trailing `Out` entry for the old event** until it detaches. On the bench's targeted
  config (`2 4 8 30 0`) that is the rest of the RestoreTrail phase plus the 30 PostGap frames, up to the next `BeginFire`.
  Their target mask is armed and filtered by membership, so those rows read `mask_state: empty` instead of `unmeasured`.
  **The delivered window does not move:** a `stuck_low_mip` event's `frame_indices` are its render-member frames only
  (`AnomalyState` source), so `Out` rows never enter the annotation.
- **One synchronous streaming update per revert** (timed). On StackOBot it is a small pass; on a heavy host it is a
  measurable game-thread hitch at every m52 revert. It is the price of fencing the streamer's plans with public API.
- **A `RESTORE TRAIL DETACHED` line per event**, normally "the next fire begins".

## 2. F1: why there is no cheaper fence (→ G348)

- `IStreamingManager::GetNumWantingResourcesID()` is documented as a generation counter, but `NumWantingResourcesCounter` has
  **no writer** under `Engine/Source/Runtime` in 5.1; only the declaration exists (`ContentStreaming.h:152`).
- `FRenderAssetStreamingManager::ProcessingStage` and `PendingMipCopyRequests` are **private**. The async task's load requests
  are reachable only through the private manager.
- `UStreamableRenderAsset::bHasStreamingUpdatePending` means `BudgetedMips > ResidentMips`, a **stream-in** signal only.
- `UpdateIndividualRenderAsset` refreshes dynamic data but applies the **stale** member `WantedMips`, so it re-issues the plan
  instead of retiring it.
- `StreamWantedMips_Internal` issues `StreamOut(WantedMips)` whenever the asset has **no** pending operation
  (`StreamingTexture.cpp:562`). That is exactly the state in which 084-02b's settled flag reads "settled".
- **Precedent for the chosen call:** the engine calls `UpdateResourceStreaming(0, true)` from `StreamAllResources`
  (`ContentStreaming.cpp:912`) and from exec paths such as `ListTextures` (`UnrealEngine.cpp:5990`) at arbitrary game-thread times.

## 6. Deviations and limitations, stated

- **F1 is fenced with a synchronous full streaming update**, the only public way to retire a 5.1 streamer plan (§2). It
  costs a game-thread pass per revert, which is measured, not assumed small. **Amortized copies stay a heuristic** bound,
  counted and documented; the engine default is off.
- **F2's grace makes a reopen after detach impossible when the detach is a capture pause.** When the detach is the next
  fire, the last in-flight receipts (about one readback latency) can still reopen a detached event. Those frames are
  flagged `products_unrecoverable` and counted, the explicit "failed qualification" Codex offered, and a reading taken while
  a new fire is live on the same target is suppressed as cross-talk. With F1's fence in place, such a late regression has
  no known in-plugin cause.
- **No target reservation during the grace.** Reserving would refuse the next targeted fire on the same target; the
  bench's targeted legs fire the same rock every burst. Detaching at `BeginFire` removes the overlap instead.
- **The monitor still stops at Revert** (Codex F4 note): a new user of a texture that is restoring after revert is not
  watched. That is outside decision 2's list and is left as a named open item.
- **Route 1 (MID texture parameter) stays undetected**, as ruled. It is in the client readme.
- **Orphan trails still use a synthetic `start_frame`** (Codex F5 provenance note, not in decision 2).
