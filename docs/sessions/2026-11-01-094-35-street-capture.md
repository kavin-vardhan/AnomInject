# 094-35: moving player-view capture on the street map

Prediction and FR3 rule, fixed before the first session: the player's current location and view target stay in use. Six poses use the starting pitch/roll and yaw offsets 0, 60, 120, 180, 240 and 300 degrees. Each transition is 18 captured frames, followed by 30 settle frames. The clean sweep holds 90 frames per pose; the scored sweep holds 480. The first settled clean-hold frame is the reference. A pixel is unstable if any RGB channel's maximum minus minimum exceeds one level over that pose's entire clean hold. Exclude those pixels from every metric. Drop a pose if its unstable share exceeds 0.05, before any scoring. Do not select references or poses by subsequent anomaly results.

The clean pre-pass has 828 frames; the scored sweep has 3,168, for 3,996 total. Auto-injection is off during warm-up, the clean sweep, turns, settling and the last 30 frames of each scored hold. The same real injector runs seed 4242 and the four smoke IDs, with 0.8-second intervals, 0.4-second holds and one concurrent event. Stopping injection at the hold boundary prevents intentional turn-edge scoring; any observed turn edges are reported separately. The first scored pass can start only after prepass-frozen.json and all six reference/unstable PNG pairs have been written. The pre-pass saves the chronology and actor-to-tag mapping of every settled clean census sample; the first such sample of an actor at that pose defines its clean silhouette. A missing silhouette fails the applicable metric rather than borrowing another pose.

The shader and streaming warm-up requires ten seconds minimum and two seconds with no pending shader jobs or wanted streaming resources, with a 120-second ceiling.
