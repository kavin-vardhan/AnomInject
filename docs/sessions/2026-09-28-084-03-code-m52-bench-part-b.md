### 2.1 Why every sync verdict is NO-JUDGEABLE-EVENT (harness limitation, not a gate result)
- The threshold is `max(5σ, 2 % pre, null worst drop)`. Tonight's nulls read **worst_drop_rel 0.266 (base) and 0.289 (fix)**,
  against **0.027** on the banked 90-frame null the harness was proven on.
- The drop is not the hold. The rock ROI's own sharpness level cycles between about 3.8 and 5.8 with a period of about six
  events ≈ 360 frames ≈ **12 s**. That matches the recorded `BP_MovingPlatform` ping-pong period (≈12.1 s, journal 045).
  The null's "worst drop" is the cycle's downslope landing at a span end, 50–60 frames after the event's first row.
  **Cause of the cycle: association with the platform period only; the platform is not established as the cause.**
- The real blur depth is similar to the bank (event 102 B0_BASE: depth 1.59, pre 4.63; bank 1.78). With thr ≈ 1.24, the
  measurability bar (3·thr ≈ 3.7) is unreachable. A 90-frame null never spans a cycle, so the harness could not see this
  in its dry runs. → **G349**.
- Consequently: no residual from the harness, the doctored-label real-pixel proof found no usable source event, and the
  `B0L_FIX` can-fail is not proven by the harness.

- AA off: none needed (0 on both edges today).

  sharpness or TAA-history convergence at the target), or a documented TAA residual. `IAI.StuckMip.SettleTailFrames` stays 0.
