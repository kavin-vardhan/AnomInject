### §2.4 🔑 WHY THE WRONG CONCLUSION WAS REACHED — the transferable part

The 21:13 control was **run at 21:12, AFTER the 21:07–21:10 failure window had already closed.**
A control that is **not concurrent** with the failures cannot control for a **time-varying** confound —
it can only tell you the system works *now*. Against seven contiguous failures it looked like a clean
A/B on the binary axis, and the arithmetic even flattered it: at `A47`'s recorded ~2-in-5 rate,
`P(7 consecutive failures) ≈ 0.4^7 ≈ 0.16 %`, which reads as "not chance" — **but that computation
silently assumes the failures are independent, and clustering is precisely the hypothesis it was being
used to dismiss.**

⇒ **`G257`.** Interleaving is not a stylistic nicety; it is what makes the comparison a comparison.
`G186` already required `A,B,B,A` ordering for *warm-up* bias in a timing prior — this is the same rule
biting on a **validity gate**, where the cost of getting it wrong is attributing a bench flake to the
product and either shipping a fix for nothing or parking a good milestone.

### §3.4 🚨 `P-C7 v3` FIRST REPORTED **FAIL**, AND THE CHECKER WAS WRONG — NOT THE BUILD

`m49b_pc7v3.py` flagged four fields differing across every pair: `frame_index` (90 rows), `t` (90),

1. **`frame_index` / `t` / `start_frame` are ABSOLUTE COUNTERS, and `P-C7 v2` explicitly rules that
   they are compared as DELTAS which must be ONE CONSTANT across every row** — comparing them for
   equality is what that ruling forbids, because two runs of the *same* binary differ by the `m41`
   startup variance. Measured: the delta is **a single constant on all 8 pairs**

   `frame_index`'s on every pair** — which is precisely right, since both are arm-time `GFrameCounter`.
2. **`mask_value` is a census-allocated stencil tag, and journal 073 already established the
   relabelling is run-to-run and BIJECTIVE.** Measured: **bijective on all 8 pairs** (e.g.
   `{227->227, 228->231, 229->247, 247->248}`). ⇒ **falsifier `F6` as I pre-declared it was too
   strong** — it demanded `mask_value` not move at all, which contradicts this project's own record.

The comparator now implements `P-C7 v2` (constant-delta + bijectivity) and reports **PASS on all 8**,
with *undeclared differing shared fields* reading **NONE — byte-identical on every shared field**.
