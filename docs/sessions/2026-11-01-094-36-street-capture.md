* Camera: 5.1 captures the view anchor at `AnomalyCaptureSubsystem.cpp:10047-10058`, but exports its legacy default far plane at 10291. * Evidence: street-session raw uint4 bytes are shuffled into 16 byte planes before zlib, with an ASP1 header; decoding reverses the operation exactly. Existing interleaved evidence remains readable. This is lossless storage only, introduced because the longer pre-pass would otherwise approach the D: floor. A kept real frame round-trips byte-for-byte (1,742,236 to 973,612 bytes in the sample).
